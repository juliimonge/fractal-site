# Getting the Amazon Storefront — Influencer Program Application Playbook

> The storefront page (`amazon.com/shop/<handle>`) comes from **one** place: approval into
> the Amazon Influencer Program. There is no other route, no paid shortcut, and Associates
> membership does not convert automatically. This is how to get approved.

**Current status:** Associates approved (`julianmonge02-20`). Influencer: not yet applied / not approved.

---

## 1 · What Amazon is actually evaluating

Amazon does not publish thresholds. What the application weighs, in rough order of importance:

| Factor | What good looks like |
|---|---|
| **Engagement rate** | The big one. Real comments and likes relative to follower count. A 2,000-follower account with 5% engagement beats a 40,000-follower account with 0.3%. |
| **Account is public** | A private account cannot be evaluated. Non-negotiable. |
| **Posting recency & consistency** | Active in the last few weeks, not dormant. |
| **Content relevance to products** | Content where physical goods plausibly appear — homes, gear, lifestyle, travel. |
| **Follower count** | Matters least of the five, but it's not zero. Commonly reported approvals sit in the low thousands; there's no published floor. |

**Your natural advantage:** real estate content is *full* of shoppable context — houses, furniture, drones, cameras, coastal living. That's a stronger relevance signal than most applicants have.

## 2 · Which platform to apply with

You can apply with **Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, or Facebook** — and you can try more than one.

| Platform | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Instagram** | Most natural fit for your content. Must be public. Business/Creator account preferred. |
| **TikTok** | Widely reported as the more forgiving path — the algorithm gives smaller accounts real reach, and Amazon sees the view counts. |
| **YouTube** | Judged on subscribers + views. If you have property walkthroughs posted, this is a strong option. |
| **Facebook** | Weakest signal generally; use as a fallback. |

**Recommended order:** try Instagram first (best content match). If declined, try TikTok, then YouTube. Each application is evaluated on its own.

## 3 · Pre-application checklist — do this BEFORE you apply

Two to three days of prep meaningfully changes the outcome. Do not apply cold.

- [ ] **Switch the account to public** (and to a Creator/Business account on Instagram)
- [ ] **Bio states who you are and where** — "Real estate in Nosara, Costa Rica" — relevance is a scored factor
- [ ] **Post 3–5 recent pieces of shoppable-adjacent content** — the gear you shoot with, a home you listed, what's on the porch, what you packed. This is exactly the content that becomes storefront material later, so nothing is wasted work
- [ ] **Reply to every comment** on recent posts — engagement rate is computed from real interaction
- [ ] **No gaps** — don't apply the week after a month of silence
- [ ] **Link julianmonge.com in the bio** — an external professional presence supports the application

## 4 · The application, step by step

1. **Sign in to Amazon** in a browser with the same account that owns `julianmonge02-20`
   *(Keeping one account = one tax profile, one payout, one set of reports.)*
2. **Switch the interface to English** — the **ES ▾** dropdown by the flag. The Influencer program is a US-marketplace program and the Spanish UI sometimes hides the entry point
3. Go to **`amazon.com/influencers`**
4. Click **Get Started / Sign up**
5. **Choose your platform** and authorize the connection (or enter the handle if prompted)
6. Amazon evaluates. **Instant approval is common**; otherwise expect up to ~5 days by email

### If the link keeps bouncing you to the Associates dashboard

That means Amazon recognized your existing account and routed you to existing tools — **it is not a rejection.** Escalate in this order:

1. The **☰ menu** on the Associates dashboard → look for *Influencer / Programa de Influencers*
2. **The Amazon mobile app** → *Account* → *Influencer Program* — the mobile flow surfaces the application most reliably when desktop doesn't
3. Sign out of Amazon entirely, then go to `amazon.com/influencers` and sign in fresh from that page
4. Try an incognito/private window (kills cached redirects)

## 5 · If you get declined

Declines are common and **not permanent**. There is no penalty and no limit on reapplying.

1. **Apply with a different platform** — TikTok or YouTube where Instagram failed. Different signals, different outcome.
2. **Grow for 30 days, reapply.** Post consistently, engage in comments, keep the account public. Most second applications after a month of real activity succeed.
3. **Your Associates account is unaffected** — tagged links keep earning the whole time. Nothing about the site build is blocked.
