## 1 · The split rule — site is the magazine, Amazon is the shelf

| | Branded site (shop.julianmonge.com) | Amazon storefront |
|---|---|---|
| Role | Editorial. Proof of taste. The brand. | Depth. The full working list. |
| Items per category | **2–4 hero picks only** | 10–20, grow freely |
| What qualifies | Things with a story you can tell in one line | Anything you'd genuinely recommend |
| Adding an item | A decision — it changes the brand | Cheap — just keep the bar at "I'd actually buy this" |
| CTA | "View on Amazon" → tagged link | native Amazon |

**Three rules:**

1. **Everything on the site is also on an Amazon list.** One-way mirror — the site is a curated window into the storefront, never a separate catalog to maintain.
2. **Commodity items never touch the site.** SD cards, cables, silica packs, surge strips — Amazon-only. They'd dilute the editorial. The site shows the camera; the list shows the 12 things that go in the bag with it.
3. **The site's own products (merch, presets, guides) never go on Amazon.** That's the one category that's site-exclusive — it's what makes the ecom worth visiting.

**What stays site-only:** Nosara merch line · Lightroom presets · PDF guides (Invest/Relocate) · content services.
**What's Amazon-only:** accessories, consumables, variants, second-choice alternatives.
**Both:** the hero pick per category.

## 6 · Julián's validation pass (required before anything ships)

Every pick above is real and well-reviewed, but the brand's entire premise is *you actually use this*. Before adding to the storefront:

- [ ] Strike anything you wouldn't personally vouch for; swap in what you actually own (especially camera/drone — if you shoot Canon, we rewrite the list around Canon)
- [ ] Check each item ships to the customer's likely address (US buyers: fine; direct-to-CR: flag items that don't ship international)
- [ ] Reprice check — "bang for buck" claims should hold at current prices
- [ ] Rewrite any note that doesn't sound like you
