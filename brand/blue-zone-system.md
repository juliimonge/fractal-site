## 1. Brand foundation

### 1.1 Name

**Primary recommendation: TUANIS**

- Costa Rican slang for *"all good / cool / nice"*. Local, distinctive, not over-saturated.
- Six letters, looks strong in caps (TUANIS), beautiful as a wordmark.
- Has a story: every product description can quietly explain it.
- Reads as a *brand*, not a *souvenir*. Distance from "Pura Vida Co." which is heavily diluted.

**Alternates worth considering:**

| Name | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|
| **NICOYA** | Geographic, Blue-Zone famous, evocative | Slightly regional vs the broader CR brief |
| **PURA** | Short, premium-feeling | Risk of reading as derivative of "Pura Vida" |
| **MONGE STANDARD** | Ties to your personal brand directly | Limits the line to your name only |
| **PACÍFICO** | Place-rooted, ocean-y | Beer brand collision |

**Recommended lockup:** `TUANIS / COSTA RICA` — wordmark + qualifier, set in caps, tracked +60. The qualifier varies: `COSTA RICA`, `NICOYA`, `PACIFIC COAST`, depending on the piece.

### 1.2 Positioning

> *"Goods from Costa Rica. Made simply, made to last."*

Not a tourist line. A real lifestyle brand from someone who actually lives here. The Pacific coast as the heritage — not the gimmick.

**Audience:**
- Visitors who fell for Costa Rica and want a piece of it home
- Expats and second-home owners on the Nicoya coast
- People who already know Blue Zones / Nicoya / surf culture
- Your real-estate clients, as a small gift / brand extension

### 1.3 Voice

- **Quiet, confident, place-rooted.** No exclamation marks. No "amazing." No emoji.
- **Specifics over adjectives.** "12oz heavyweight cotton, garment-dyed in Sand." beats "Premium quality tee, you'll love it."
- **Spanish where it earns its place.** Don't sprinkle it for flavor. `TUANIS`, `PURA VIDA`, `NICOYA` are right. `Hola amigo, vibes!` is wrong.
- **First-person sparingly.** "Made here. Sold here." not "I made this and I love it."

### 1.4 What the brand is not

- Not "tourist-shop bright". No surf-pop pastels stacked together. No cartoon parrots.
- Not "luxury for luxury's sake". No gold foil, no marble textures.
- Not "fast" in any sense — not fast fashion, not fast voice, not trendy typography.

## 2. The Blue Zone design system

### 2.1 Principles

The five Blue Zones share patterns of longevity. The brand borrows those patterns as design rules:

| Principle | What it means for design |
|---|---|
| **Longevity over trend** | Designs read good in 10 years. Avoid trendy fonts, trendy palettes. |
| **Place over generic** | Geographic specificity earns the design — Nicoya, Guiones, coordinates, the peninsula outline. Generic "tropical paradise" doesn't. |
| **Plant-forward palette** | Earth tones taken from the actual landscape — sand, teal water, terracotta clay, jungle green. No neon. |
| **Quiet confidence** | Typography does the work. Restraint over decoration. Empty space respected. |
| **Made with intent** | Production sourced for weight and longevity — heavyweight cotton, real fleece, embroidery over print where it fits. |

### 2.2 Color tokens

Drawn from the landscape — water, sand, jungle, sunset, dry-season grass.

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `--ink` | `#0E1A20` | Primary text, primary print on light fabric |
| `--deep-teal` | `#0C2830` | Primary brand color, dark fabric base |
| `--ocean` | `#17617C` | Secondary brand, mid-tone, accent prints |
| `--light-teal` | `#9FC8D0` | Accent, light-fabric prints on dark, calls to action |
| `--sand` | `#E8DFC8` | Garment color (tee/tote base), light surfaces |
| `--cream` | `#F6F2E8` | Page background, the "paper" tone |
| `--terracotta` | `#C2725A` | Sunset accent, used sparingly on tags/badges |
| `--jungle` | `#3D5C3A` | Wet-season green, used sparingly |
| `--gold` | `#C9A875` | Heritage accent, dry-season grass, embroidery thread |
| `--white` | `#FFFFFF` | Reverses on dark fabric |

**Pairing rules:**
- Two-color print maximum on apparel (one dark + one light, or one ink + one accent).
- Embroidery: maximum 3 thread colors per design.
- Never combine `--terracotta` and `--jungle` in the same artwork (clashes).
- `--gold` reads well on `--deep-teal` and `--ink`. Avoid on `--sand` (low contrast).

### 2.3 Typography

We use two free-to-license families so production is unblocked:

**Display / wordmark — Inter (700, 800)**
- Tracked +0.08em to +0.18em in caps for wordmarks
- Used for product labels, tags, hero headlines
- Never italic

**Editorial / body — Inter (300, 400, 500)**
- Used for product descriptions, copy, web body
- Line height 1.55 for body, 1.2 for headlines

**Optional moments — Playfair Display (500, italic)**
- Used very sparingly, one italic word per moment, never an entire heading
- Example: *"Pura vida."* as a quiet pull-quote in editorial

**On-garment type only — Inter Bold (700)** at the sizes specified per product. No serif on garments. Keep type radically simple on fabric.

### 2.4 Photography direction

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Real Nicoya: Guiones, Pelada, Garza, jungle, road | Generic "tropical beach" stock |
| Documentary, candid, golden hour | Over-saturated, over-edited |
| People mid-activity (surfing, walking, cooking) | Posed model shots |
| 4:5 portrait for product, 3:2 landscape for editorial | Square unless on social |
| Subtle film grain OK | Heavy filters, vignettes |

**Lightroom treatment:** use the *Nosara Coastal Lightroom Preset Pack* presets shipped in `lightroom-presets/Nosara Coastal/` for consistent grading across all marketing imagery. Start with **01 Golden Hour** or **05 Interior Warm** for product flat lays; **04 Coastal Surf** for environment shots.

### 2.5 Logo system

Three lockups, all built from the same wordmark:

**1. Primary lockup — `TUANIS / COSTA RICA`**
```
TUANIS / COSTA RICA
```
Inter 800 caps, "TUANIS" at 100% size, " / " in 400 weight at 60% opacity, "COSTA RICA" at 60% size and 500 weight with +0.24em tracking.

**2. Tag lockup — `TUANIS`**
```
TUANIS
```
Standalone wordmark for woven labels, embroidery patches, sticker centers.

**3. Place lockup — `TUANIS · NICOYA` or `TUANIS · GUIONES`**
```
TUANIS · NICOYA
```
Used on caps, the second half is interchangeable per drop or per-location piece. The `·` is a true middle dot (U+00B7).

### 2.6 Iconography (sparingly)

A small kit of geographic/heritage motifs used across artwork. Each is line-art only, single weight, no fills.

- **Nicoya peninsula outline** — vector tracing of the peninsula, 1.5px line weight at 100mm width
- **Coordinates mark** — small caps "10.0°N · 85.5°W" with leading dash
- **Three-line sun** — three nested arcs over a horizon line, geometric
- **Surfboard** — single-line silhouette, no fin detail
- **Macaw silhouette** — one-color, flying profile, no eye detail
- **Coffee bean** — geometric, side view, single line

These are *motifs*, not a sticker pack. Used at small scale as supporting marks, not centerpieces.

### 2.7 Layout grid

For web and printed collateral:
- 12-column grid, 1.5rem gutters
- Max content width: 1440px
- Section padding: 5rem top/bottom desktop, 3.5rem mobile

For garment artwork, see per-product specs below.

### 2.8 Don'ts

- No drop shadows on type
- No gradient fills on type
- No emoji anywhere — including product descriptions
- No exclamation marks
- No "we" / "us" / "the team" — single voice, even if multiple people work on it
- No "limited edition" unless it actually is limited

## 4. Artwork briefs (per design)

Every artwork needs to be supplied to Printful in two forms when the placement is on multiple fabric tones:
1. **Dark-on-light** (for Sand, Cream, Ecru, Natural colorways)
2. **Light-on-dark** (for Deep Teal colorways)

**File format requirements:**
- **For DTG print:** PNG, transparent background, 300 DPI, sRGB, 4500 × 5400 px (covers any apparel placement at full size)
- **For embroidery:** Vector SVG or AI file with stroke and fill clearly distinguished. Max 3 thread colors. No gradients, no soft edges. Minimum line weight 1.5mm at final size.
- **For sticker / mug sublimation:** PNG, 300 DPI, transparent background, sized to the specified placement dimensions plus 3mm bleed on each edge

**File naming convention (for Printful upload):**
```
TUANIS_<artwork-id>_<variant>_<placement>.<ext>

Examples:
TUANIS_A_light_chest.png         (Artwork A, light-fabric version, chest placement)
TUANIS_C_embroidery.svg          (Artwork C, embroidery vector)
TUANIS_F_sleeve.png              (Artwork F, sleeve runner, single variant)
```

## 5. Printful production workflow

Step-by-step, assuming you already have a Shopify store and a Printful account connected.

### 5.1 One-time setup

1. **Printful account:** Sign up at [printful.com](https://printful.com). Free.
2. **Connect to Shopify:** Printful → *Stores* → *Add new store* → *Shopify* → authenticate. Already covered in the existing theme `README.md`.
3. **Set up billing:** Printful → *Billing* → add card. Printful charges per order when a sale happens; no upfront cost.
4. **Choose default branding:**
   - Printful → *Branding* → upload your *outside packaging sticker* (TUANIS roundel) and *inside packing slip header*
   - This is what arrives in the customer's box. Make it match the brand.

### 5.2 Per-product creation flow

For each SKU above, do this once:

1. **Printful → Catalog → choose the blank** specified in the product spec (e.g. "Comfort Colors C1717").
2. **Select print method:** DTG, Embroidery, or Sublimation as specified.
3. **Upload the artwork file:**
   - Click the placement (e.g. *Front print*)
   - Upload the PNG or SVG you produced per the artwork brief
   - For multi-fabric-tone products, you need two product variants: one with the light-fabric artwork, one with the dark-fabric artwork. Either:
     - **Option A (cleaner):** Create one Printful product per artwork variant, then list as separate Shopify products
     - **Option B (denser):** Use Printful's "Conditional artwork" feature where available, attaching different artwork to different color variants of the same product
4. **Position the artwork** using Printful's placement tool. The on-screen rulers show actual mm — match the placement specs above exactly.
5. **Generate mockups:** Printful auto-generates flat mockups. Use *Lifestyle* mockups for the hero product image, *Flat* for the variant grid. Override with your own photography when you have it.
6. **Set sizes and colors enabled:** match the product spec table.
7. **Set the retail price** (Printful → *Pricing*) to the suggested price. Printful shows the wholesale cost; the difference is your margin.
8. **Set SKU** to the format `TU-<product>-<color>-<size>` e.g. `TU-TEE-HW-SAND-M`.
9. **Push to Shopify:** Click *Submit to store*. The product appears as a draft in Shopify.
10. **In Shopify admin:** open the product → assign to collections (`Tuanis Line`, `Apparel`, etc.) → add SEO meta from the description copy → publish.

### 5.3 Embroidery-specific notes

- Artwork must be a vector file (SVG or AI), not a raster image.
- Printful's automatic digitization is fine for simple designs (1–2 colors, large shapes). For Artwork C, D — submit and let Printful auto-digitize.
- Order **one sample per embroidered product** before listing publicly. Embroidery is the highest-failure print method and the on-screen mockup never perfectly matches the stitched result.

### 5.4 Sublimation (mug) notes

- The mug template is 240 × 95mm flat. The artwork wraps the full circumference, so the LEFT and RIGHT edges of your file must align perfectly to look seamless on the curved surface. The Printful template has a "seam alignment" indicator — keep the wordmark away from the seam line.

### 5.5 Mockup photography (longer term)

Printful's auto-generated mockups work for launch. For your second drop, shoot real photography:

- Hire a local Nosara photographer (or shoot yourself with the Lightroom presets pack)
- Flat-lay each garment on the same neutral background (Sand or Cream surface)
- One model shot per garment on coast, in golden hour
- Replace Printful mockups in Shopify admin once shoots are done
