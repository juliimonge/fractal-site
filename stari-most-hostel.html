01 / The Hostel

A family welcome
in the old town

Tucked into the heart of Mostar on Braće Fejića, Hostel Backpackers is a family-run house just a two-minute walk from the legendary Old Bridge (Stari Most) and the lively Kujundžiluk bazaar where coppersmiths still hammer by hand.

Spread across two buildings, we keep it simple and warm: air-conditioned rooms, a self-catering kitchen, a shared lounge, and big windows that catch the call to prayer at dawn and the river light at dusk. The main house is crowned by a rooftop bar & terrace made for golden hour.

It's the easy warmth of a family guesthouse, steps from everything worth seeing in the old town — with staff who'll point you to the best waterfalls, cliffs and rafting runs in the valley.

03 / The Rooftop

Golden hour,
every evening

The reason guests come back. Our rooftop bar & terrace floats above the tiled roofs of the old town, with the minarets, the mountains and the Neretva laid out in front of you. It's the best seat in Mostar for sunset — and it happens to be upstairs from your bed.

Pour a drink at the bar, settle into a low cushion as the call to prayer drifts across the valley, and trade travel stories with new friends under the string lights.
